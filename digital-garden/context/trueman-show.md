# Truman Show

A man grows up a world surrounded by family, friends, and people he loves, only to find out the world was created specifically for him by a reality TV show director. His whole life has been a lie, and was used for entertainment. \
He later finds out, and leaves this world. Staying in would be easy, but going out is important.
