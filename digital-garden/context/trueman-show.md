date: 2026-07-15

summary: Very brief summary of the Trueman show.
---

A man grows up in a world surrounded by family, friends, and people he loves, only to find out this world was created specifically for him by a reality TV show director. His entire life has been a lie, used by the people involed for entertainment. \
He later finds out, and decides to leave this world. Staying in is easy and comforting, but that is a life of lies.
