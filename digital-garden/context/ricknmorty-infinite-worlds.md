---

Rick&Morty "The Ricks must be Crazy" (S2E6, Batteries), "Fear No Mort" (S7E10, best) and the "Inception" have a somewhat common angle, of worlds inside of worlds.

The Batteries episode, has worlds that are real at all levels. A child world here is created by a citizen of the parent world, for the same purpose. \
Fear No Mort has worlds which are not exactly real, but we have no way to distinguish between a child and a parent world.
