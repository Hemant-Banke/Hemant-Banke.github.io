# Digital Gardens

A **digital garden** rejects the blog's tyranny of chronology. Posts aren't a
feed to be consumed newest-first; they're plants at different stages of growth,
cross-linked into a living map.

## Principles

- **Grown, not published.** Notes keep growing with thoughts.
- **Topology over timeline.** The [[On Tools for Thought|link]] is the unit.
- **Public by default.** Thinking out loud invites better thinking.

A well-connected corpus is more useful than a big pile of documents.

## Why a graph view

If links are the point, you should be able to *see* them. That's the whole
argument for the graph: the folder structure and the links are real containers 
and edges, so both deserve to be drawn.
