RL will keep improving. World Models create an environment for training. Realistic World Models are necessary for accurate simulations of reality, in-turn leading to better training and more possible experiments. \
If we had an accurate simulation of reality in form of this model, we will have endless possibilities of training and experimentation. We can create policies and agents that behave as they should, trained over billions of iterations in a reality indifferent from ours, in a meaningfully small time compared to what it would have taken in our irreversible world. \
Its existence is very important, and will prove to be incredibly beneficial.
